## Thiruvonam in Rogaland

Onam is the soul of Kerala, and every year ARK brings that soul to Rogaland. The harvest festival — celebrated across caste, religion, and background — is a reminder that home is not just a place, it is a feeling. And for Keralites in Stavanger and Sandnes, ARK's Onam celebration is that feeling made real.

## The Grand Sadya

The highlight of our Onam is the traditional **Sadya** — the legendary Kerala feast served on a banana leaf with over 20 dishes, from avial and olan to payasam. Every dish is lovingly prepared by our community members, carrying recipes and memories from kitchens back home.

## Cultural Programme

The evening unfolds with a rich cultural programme:

- **Pookalam** (floral carpet) competition — create your own design and win prizes
- **Thiruvathira** dance performance by ARK members
- **Classical and folk music** from our talented community
- **Children's cultural items** — the little ones always steal the show
- **Fancy dress** — come dressed as a King or Queen of Mahabali's kingdom!
