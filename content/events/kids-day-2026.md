## A Day Made for Our Little Ones

Kids Day is one of ARK's most beloved annual traditions — a full day of joy, laughter, and Kerala warmth here in Stavanger. Every year, we gather at LuraHallen to celebrate the children of our community with activities that blend the best of Kerala culture with the spirit of a Norwegian summer.

## What to Expect

The day is packed with activities for all ages:

- **Cultural performances** — traditional dance, music, and skits performed by our children
- **Games and sports** — both indoor and outdoor activities suited for all age groups
- **Kerala food stalls** — enjoy authentic snacks and meals prepared by our community members
- **Art and craft corner** — a dedicated space for the youngest ones to paint and create
- **Prize distribution** — recognising our children's achievements throughout the year

## Practical Details

- **Date:** Sunday, 14 June 2026
- **Venue:** LuraHallen, Stavanger
- **Time:** 11:00 – 17:00
- **Entry:** Free for ARK members; small contribution for non-members

All children are welcome. Bring the whole family!
